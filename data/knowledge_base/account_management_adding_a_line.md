# Adding a Line

Adding a line usually requires the account holder to choose a plan, confirm device or SIM availability, and review any activation charges or monthly recurring fees. Support should explain whether the new line will share a family plan, have its own number, or require a device installment agreement. Customers often want to know how soon the line will be active and whether they need to restart the device after activation.

Before completing the order, the agent should confirm any changes to the total monthly bill and whether promotional pricing applies only for a limited time. If the customer is adding a line for a child, roommate, or business user, it can also help to review usage controls and line-level permissions. Clear expectations reduce callbacks after the order is placed.
