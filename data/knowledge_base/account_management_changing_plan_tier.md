# Changing Plan Tier

Customers can usually move to a higher or lower plan tier when their usage needs change. A downgrade may reduce monthly cost but also lower speed, data allowance, or included features, while an upgrade may unlock more capacity or premium support. Agents should explain the billing impact before submitting the change so the customer knows whether the new rate begins immediately or at the next bill cycle.

It is also important to review any promotions tied to the current plan. A customer who changes tiers may lose a temporary discount, device credit, or bundled benefit. If the account includes multiple lines, the agent should confirm whether the plan change applies to the whole account or only to a specific line.
