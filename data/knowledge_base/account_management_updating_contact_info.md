# Updating Contact Information

Customers should update their contact information whenever they change a phone number, email address, or billing address. Keeping the account current ensures that outage notices, billing reminders, and security alerts reach the right person. Support should verify the account holder before making the change and then confirm which contact fields were updated.

If the customer is updating a billing address, the agent should also confirm whether the service address is changing or whether the mailing address is different from the physical location. That distinction matters for taxes, service eligibility, and emergency notifications. Once the update is saved, the customer should be told where to verify the change in the portal or app.
