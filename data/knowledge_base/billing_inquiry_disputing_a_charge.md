# Disputing a Charge

If a customer believes a charge is incorrect, the first step is to identify the exact invoice and the item being disputed. Common examples include duplicate fees, equipment that was already returned, or a plan change that was never authorized. Billing disputes are easier to resolve when the customer can provide the charge date, amount, and the reason they believe it should be removed.

Most disputes are reviewed against service records, order history, and payment activity. If the charge is valid but unexpected, support can explain what triggered it and whether any prorated amount, late fee, or add-on service was applied. If the charge is found to be an error, the account can usually be adjusted or credited according to the company billing policy.
