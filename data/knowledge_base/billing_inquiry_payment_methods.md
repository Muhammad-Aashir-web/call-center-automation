# Accepted Payment Methods

Most customers can pay by debit card, major credit card, ACH bank transfer, or by making a payment through the customer portal or mobile app. Some accounts also support autopay, which can help avoid late fees and keep service active without manual monthly payments. Payment options may vary slightly for business accounts, prepaid services, or accounts with past-due balances.

If a payment fails, the most common causes are an expired card, an incorrect billing ZIP code, or a bank that blocks the transaction for security reasons. Customers should update the payment method as soon as possible and confirm that autopay is re-enabled if they want recurring billing to continue. A support agent can also confirm whether a payment is pending, posted, or returned by the bank.
