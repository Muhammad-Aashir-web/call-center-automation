# Understanding Your Bill

Your monthly statement is split into service charges, device or equipment charges, taxes, and any one-time adjustments. If the amount looks higher than expected, compare the current bill to the previous month and check for prorated charges, late fees, or plan changes that took effect mid-cycle. In most cases, the bill summary will show whether the total is driven by a new service activation, an upgrade, or a balance carried over from an earlier invoice.

Customers should review the detailed charge breakdown before calling support. If something still looks wrong, note the invoice date, the charge amount, and the specific line item so a billing specialist can review it faster. Having the last four digits of the payment method and the service address available also helps confirm the account quickly.
