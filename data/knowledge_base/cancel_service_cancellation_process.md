# Cancellation Process

To cancel service, the customer typically needs to confirm the account holder information, the service address, and the requested cancellation date. Support should explain whether the cancellation can be processed immediately or whether the account must remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. Customers should also be told to return any rented equipment to avoid unreturned-device fees.

A clear cancellation conversation should also cover what happens after the disconnect date. That includes final billing, autopay status, and whether the customer wants to keep any email or paper statements for records. If the customer is moving, agents can confirm whether a move transfer or address change would be a better fit than a full cancellation.
