# Early Termination Fees

An early termination fee may apply when a customer cancels a contract before the agreed term ends. The exact charge depends on the remaining months, the service agreement, and whether any promotional credits or device financing were included. Support should explain how the fee is calculated instead of simply quoting a flat amount, because customers often need to understand why the final balance is higher than expected.

If a customer says they were not told about the fee, the agent should review the original order notes, the contract term, and any promotional disclosures. In some situations, a save offer, contract extension, or alternate plan may reduce the need to cancel right away. If the fee is valid, the customer should still be told when it will appear and whether it must be paid before the account closes.
