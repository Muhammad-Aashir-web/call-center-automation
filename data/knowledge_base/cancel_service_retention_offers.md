# Retention Offers

When a customer calls to cancel, retention teams may offer a lower-rate plan, a temporary discount, or a service upgrade at a reduced price. These offers are usually designed to address the reason the customer wants to leave, such as price pressure, poor performance, or a competitor promotion. The customer should never feel pressured; the goal is to present the available options clearly and let them decide.

Retention offers should be explained with the total monthly cost, any term commitment, and whether the price changes after an introductory period. If the customer still prefers to cancel, the agent should proceed with the disconnect request and avoid delaying the process. A respectful handoff matters more than pushing an offer that does not solve the customer's concern.
