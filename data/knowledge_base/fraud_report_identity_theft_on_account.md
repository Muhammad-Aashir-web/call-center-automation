# Identity Theft on an Account

Identity theft concerns usually involve a customer saying someone opened or modified an account without permission. Agents should verify the caller, lock down the account if policy allows, and review recent changes such as address updates, password resets, or new line activations. The most important step is to stop additional changes while the investigation is in progress.

The customer should be told which documents or proof may be needed to confirm ownership and restore control of the account. Depending on the situation, the account may need to be flagged for fraud review and monitored for additional unauthorized activity. Prompt action helps reduce billing loss and protects the customer from further misuse.
