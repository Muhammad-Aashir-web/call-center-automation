# Phishing Scam Awareness

Phishing scams often try to trick customers into sharing passwords, one-time codes, or payment information through fake emails, texts, or phone calls. Support should remind customers that legitimate service teams will not ask for sensitive credentials in an unsolicited message. If the customer clicked a suspicious link, they should change their password and review recent account activity immediately.

Customers should also be advised to look for signs such as mismatched sender addresses, urgent payment threats, or requests to install unknown software. When a scam is reported, the account may need a security note so future agents know to watch for suspicious activity. Educating customers on these warning signs is one of the best ways to prevent repeat fraud attempts.
