# Contacting Support

Customers can usually reach support by phone, chat, email, or self-service depending on the type of issue and the time of day. The best contact method often depends on whether the customer needs immediate troubleshooting or just wants to update account information. Support should explain expected wait times and which channels are available for after-hours assistance.

If the issue is urgent, such as a total service outage or a possible security problem, the customer should be routed to the fastest available channel. For routine questions, the customer may be better served by the portal or help center. Giving the customer the right channel the first time reduces repeat contacts and lowers frustration.
