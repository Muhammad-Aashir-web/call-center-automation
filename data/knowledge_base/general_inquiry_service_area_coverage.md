# Service Area Coverage

Before promising service, agents should check the customer’s exact address against the service availability tool. Coverage can vary by street, building, or unit, especially in newer developments and rural areas. If service is not currently available, the agent should explain whether there is a waitlist, a future expansion plan, or an alternative product such as wireless home internet.

Customers often ask whether coverage includes specific speeds or features. In those cases, it is important to distinguish between availability and actual performance, since the service tier and local line conditions may affect what the customer experiences. Accurate coverage answers prevent order cancellations and unrealistic expectations later.
