# Store Hours and Locations

Customers looking for store hours should be directed to the current location listing because holiday schedules, staffing changes, and seasonal hours can vary by site. Support should confirm which store the customer wants, since many areas have multiple retail locations with different hours and services. If the customer needs in-person help, it is useful to mention whether the store handles billing, device pickup, technical swaps, or account changes.

When a store is nearby but closed, customers may still be able to complete many tasks through the portal, app, or phone support. For busy locations, it can help to mention whether appointments are accepted or whether walk-ins are first come, first served. Clear location details save the customer a second trip.
