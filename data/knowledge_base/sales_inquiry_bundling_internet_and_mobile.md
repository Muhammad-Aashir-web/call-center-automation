# Bundling Internet and Mobile

Bundle offers usually combine home internet and mobile service at a lower total cost than buying each product separately. When explaining a bundle, the agent should outline which discounts require both services to remain active and whether the promotional rate changes after a trial period. Customers often want to know if their existing mobile line can be moved into the bundle without changing numbers or devices.

It is also useful to explain how billing works when two services are combined. Some bundles produce one invoice, while others still show separate charges with a shared discount. Clear details about eligibility, activation steps, and monthly savings help the customer compare the bundle against their current setup.
