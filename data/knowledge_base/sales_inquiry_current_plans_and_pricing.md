# Current Plans and Pricing

When customers ask about plans, the agent should focus on the core differences: monthly price, speed or data allotment, included features, and any contract requirement. Pricing can change based on promotions, bundle eligibility, and whether equipment is rented or owned. The most helpful answer is one that compares a few realistic options instead of only quoting the lowest advertised price.

Customers should also be told whether taxes, fees, or autopay discounts affect the final monthly total. If a promotion is limited time only, that should be made clear before the customer decides. Transparent pricing reduces repeat calls and lowers the chance of billing surprises after activation.
