# Upgrade Options

Customers looking for an upgrade may want faster internet, a larger data plan, a newer device, or add-on features such as security or premium support. Agents should explain which upgrades are immediately available and whether a device trade-in, new contract, or financing agreement is required. The best sales conversation balances the customer’s needs with a clear view of the recurring monthly cost.

If the customer already has service, the agent should confirm whether upgrading will change the current billing cycle or promotional pricing. In some cases, an upgrade can be applied right away; in others, it may begin at the next statement date. Giving the customer a full picture of the timing and price helps prevent confusion after the order is placed.
