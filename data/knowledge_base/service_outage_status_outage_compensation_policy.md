# Outage Compensation Policy

Customers often ask whether they will receive a credit for a service outage. The usual policy is that compensation depends on the length of the interruption, the type of service affected, and whether the outage was caused by a network event or customer-side equipment. In many cases, short interruptions do not qualify for automatic credit, while longer verified outages may be eligible for a prorated adjustment.

Agents should explain that credits are typically reviewed after the service is restored and the outage ticket is closed. Customers should be encouraged to document the start time, end time, and any visible outage notices they received. That information helps billing confirm whether the account qualifies under the current outage credit policy.
