# No Dial Tone Troubleshooting

If a customer has no dial tone, confirm that the phone is plugged into the correct jack and that any home wiring splitter or adapter is firmly seated. For voice-over-IP service, the modem or voice gateway should be powered on, connected to the network, and showing normal service lights before the phone line will work. A power cycle of the gateway often restores dial tone after an outage or equipment reset.

Agents should also ask whether the issue affects one handset or every phone in the home. If only one handset is affected, the problem is usually local to the device, cord, or wall jack. If every phone is silent, the account may have a service interruption, a provisioning issue, or a gateway that needs to be remotely refreshed.
